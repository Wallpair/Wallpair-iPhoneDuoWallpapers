Getting Started

WALLPAIR provides curated wallpaper pairs. Each Wall Pair includes:

  • A Lock Screen wallpaper (4:3).
  • A Home Screen wallpaper (4:3).

To use a Wall Pair:

  1. Choose a Wall Pair in the app and Collect it.
  2. WALLPAIR saves both photos to Recents.
  3. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Add, then Photos. Choose the Lock Screen photo.
  4. After Add, tap Customize Home Screen and set the Home Screen photo.

On iPhone Duo, the same Lock Screen and Home Screen pair is made for that device.

Photos Permission

WALLPAIR may request permission to save the Lock Screen and Home Screen wallpapers you collect to your Photos library.

WALLPAIR requests add-only access. It does not access, read, or modify your existing photos.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

Collected Wall Pairs are in YOUR WALLPAIRS.

To save a pair to Photos again:

  1. Open YOUR WALLPAIRS.
  2. Choose the Wall Pair.
  3. Collect it again, or follow the in-app prompt to save Lock Screen and Home Screen to Photos.

Use Restore Purchases in Help & Support if eligible purchases are missing after reinstalling or changing devices.

Widgets

WALLPAIR widgets are optional and are added to Home Screen. Touch and hold Home Screen, tap Edit, tap Add Widget, and choose WALLPAIR.

Depending on the widgets you use, WALLPAIR may request:

  • Location, for local weather in the Weather widget (after you collect a Premium Wall Pair).
  • Apple Reminders, to show and complete tasks in the To Do widget.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on, for optional app alerts.

You can change or withdraw these permissions in Settings.
